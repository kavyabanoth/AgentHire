Data Analyst - Entry Level

We are looking for a Data Analyst to join our analytics team. The ideal
candidate has strong skills in Python and SQL, with hands-on experience
building dashboards in Power BI or Tableau.

Responsibilities:
- Analyze large datasets to identify trends and business insights
- Build and maintain interactive dashboards for stakeholders
- Write efficient SQL queries to extract and transform data
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define KPIs
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders

Required Skills:
- Python (pandas, numpy)
- SQL
- Data visualization (Power BI or Tableau)
- Strong communication skills

Preferred Skills:
- Experience with machine learning basics (scikit-learn)
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS/Azure)
- Knowledge of A/B testing and statistical analysis

Education: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Engineering,
or a related field.

Experience: 0-2 years (internships count).
